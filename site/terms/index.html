Plain terms for

Effective 28 August 2026

The product

Dictation Repair Book is a local utility that proposes text-replacement rules from corrections you provide. Review suggestions before relying on them, especially for medical, legal, safety-critical, or production commands. It is not a transcription, medical, or accessibility service.

Free and paid use

The free app includes 25 approved rules, testing, and all exports. A $24 one-time personal license unlocks unlimited approved rules, plus updates through version 1.x. Sociobot/Dodo is merchant of record. Refunds are handled there and revoke the associated license.

Your data and responsibility

You retain ownership of your vocabulary and exports. You are responsible for permission to process workplace or personal text and for keeping exported backups. We do not receive the local vault and cannot recover an erased vault or lost local key.
